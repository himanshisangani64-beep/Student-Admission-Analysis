## 📌 Project Overview

This project uses **Microsoft Excel** to analyze student admission data and explore admission patterns across branches, age groups, courses, admission modes, and admission sources.

The dashboard combines KPI cards, charts, summary tables, and interactive filters to transform admission records into meaningful insights.

It provides a consolidated view of total admissions, student counts, branch distribution, course preferences, and the channels through which students apply for admission.

## 🎯 Project Objective

- Analyze total student admissions and student counts.
- Compare admissions across different branches and locations.
- Understand the age distribution of admitted students.
- Analyze online and offline admission modes.
- Identify popular courses based on admission counts.
- Compare admission sources such as LinkedIn, websites, referrals, and advertisements.
- Review monthly admission patterns.
- Build an interactive Excel dashboard for admission reporting and analysis.

## ✨ Key Features

- Total Admission KPI
- Total Student KPI
- Total Branch KPI
- Year-wise selection for 2025 and 2026
- Month-wise admission filtering
- Branch-based interactive filters
- Branch area-wise admission distribution
- Age-wise total admission analysis
- Online versus offline admission comparison
- Course-wise admission analysis
- Admission source comparison
- Admission summary table
- Highest and lowest value summaries
- Multiple charts and KPI cards
- Consolidated dashboard for admission analysis

## 📊 Analysis & Excel Practice

### 1. 📅 Month & Year Analysis

Used month and year selection controls to explore admission records across different periods and compare admission activity.

### 2. 🏢 Branch-wise Admission Analysis

Compared admissions across branch areas such as Vesu, A.K. Road, Yogi Chowk, Mota Varachha, and Ved Road.

### 3. 🎂 Age-wise Admission Analysis

Analyzed the number of admissions by student age to understand the distribution of admitted students across age groups.

### 4. 💻 Admission Mode Analysis

Compared online and offline admissions to understand the distribution of admission methods.

### 5. 📚 Course-wise Admission Analysis

Compared admissions across courses such as CorelDRAW, UI/UX & Graphic Design, AI/ML & Data Science, Graphic Design & Video Editing, and other listed courses.

### 6. 📢 Admission Source Analysis

Analyzed admission sources, including LinkedIn, Website, Branch Visit, Friend Referral, WhatsApp, College Seminar, Ads, and Family Referral.

### 7. 📋 Admission Summary

Used the summary table to review the highest and lowest values for the displayed admission dimensions.

### 8. 📈 Dashboard & Data Visualization

Combined KPI cards, a doughnut chart, bar charts, a pie chart, a summary table, and interactive filters into one dashboard for clear reporting.

## 🔄 From Raw Data to Admission Insights

This project follows a structured workflow to transform student admission records into useful information.

| **Step** | **Process** | **Purpose** |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Raw Data | Explore student admission records |
| 02 | Data Preparation | Organize student, branch, course, and admission details |
| 03 | KPI Analysis | Summarize total admissions, students, and branches |
| 04 | Admission Analysis | Compare age, course, branch, and admission source |
| 05 | Data Visualization | Create charts and summary tables |
| 06 | Dashboard Creation | Combine KPIs, charts, and interactive filters |
| 07 | Admission Insights | Identify admission patterns and course preferences |
